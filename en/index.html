Machete by LMM SOLUTIONS

Your real Amazon profit, per product and per day.

Machete is a web application published by LMM SOLUTIONS, a company based in France, for Amazon FBA and FBM sellers in Europe. It shows the seller their real net profit per product and per day, after Amazon fees, advertising, product costs and VAT.

Try the free demo Open the app

The app is in French. This page summarizes it in English.

How it works today

Upload your Amazon reports, read your margin.

1. Upload your reports

Sellers download their reports from Amazon Seller Central (monthly transactions, tax report) and upload the files to Machete.

2. Read the results

  • Net margin per SKU, per marketplace and per month
  • VAT tracking
  • Monthly profitability reports

The app is available at app.lmm-solutions.com, with a free demo account.

Coming next

Connecting the Amazon account directly.

Next, sellers will be able to connect their Amazon account through Amazon's official Selling Partner API, instead of uploading files. This is not available yet.

Data and privacy

Each seller's data stays theirs.

  • Each seller sees only their own data.
  • No buyer names, addresses, emails or phone numbers are stored.
  • Amazon data is never sold or shared.
  • The seller can ask us to delete their account and all their data at any time.
  • Hosting in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
